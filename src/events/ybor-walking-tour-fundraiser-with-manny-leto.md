Hispanic Heritage Month Fundraising Walking Tour

## Walk the City Within a City

Celebrate Hispanic Heritage Month with a guided walking tour of historic Ybor City in support of Taryn Sabia for Mayor. Tampa native **Manny Leto**, Executive Director of Preserve the ’Burg, will lead an approximately one-mile walk exploring the cigar factories, social clubs, and local businesses that helped build Tampa. Discover the stories behind one of our city’s most distinctive neighborhoods.

Saturday, October 3, 2026 10:00 AM Historic Ybor City Approximately 1 mile

Suggested contribution: $30
