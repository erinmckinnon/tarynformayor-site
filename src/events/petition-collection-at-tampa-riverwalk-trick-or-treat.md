## Petition Collection at Tampa Riverwalk Trick or Treat

Saturday, October 24, 2026 • 4:00–7:00 PM

Join Team Taryn Sabia to help collect petition signatures at Tampa Riverwalk Trick or Treat. Spend a three-hour volunteer shift connecting with residents and supporting Taryn’s campaign for Tampa Mayor.

**Cotanchobee Fort Brooke Park**
Downtown Tampa
Contact the team for the volunteer meeting point.

To volunteer, email [contact@tarynformayor.com](mailto:contact@tarynformayor.com) and let us know you’d like to help collect petition signatures at Riverwalk Trick or Treat on October 24.
