## Register to Vote
Deadline

Registration deadline: February 1, 2027

Make sure you’re ready to vote in Tampa’s upcoming municipal election on **March 2, 2027**. If you are not already registered to vote in Florida, submit your voter registration application by **February 1, 2027**.

Already registered? Take a moment to review your voter information and make sure your name and address are current.

[Register to Vote Online](https://registertovoteflorida.gov/) [Registration & Update Information](https://www.votehillsborough.gov/163/Register-or-Update-Information)

Visit the Hillsborough County Supervisor of Elections website for registration requirements and instructions for submitting your application.
