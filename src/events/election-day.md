## Election Day
in Tampa

Make a plan to vote on Election Day. Find your assigned polling location and review official voting information from the Hillsborough County Supervisor of Elections before heading to the polls.

Polls open: 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM

[Find My Polling Location](https://www.votehillsborough.gov/159/Voting-Precincts) [Election Day Voting Information](https://www.votehillsborough.gov/170/Election-Day)

On Election Day, vote at the polling place assigned to your precinct. If you are in line by 7:00 PM, you will be allowed to vote.
