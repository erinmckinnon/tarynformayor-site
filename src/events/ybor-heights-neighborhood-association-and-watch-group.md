Taryn Sabia will be attending the Ybor Heights Neighborhood Association meeting to connect with residents and listen to the priorities of the community. These meetings provide an opportunity for neighbors to engage directly with local leaders and candidates, ask questions, and share what matters most in their neighborhood.

The Ybor Heights Neighborhood Association welcomes all candidates as part of their commitment to helping residents make informed decisions. While the association does not endorse candidates, they provide a valuable space for open conversation and community input.

Residents are encouraged to attend, meet Taryn, and be part of the discussion about the future of Tampa.
