Join us for the official kickoff of Taryn Sabia’s campaign for Mayor of Tampa. Set against the historic backdrop of one of Tampa’s most iconic civic spaces, this cocktail-style gathering will bring together community leaders, supporters, and neighbors to celebrate the launch of a bold, people-centered vision for Tampa’s future. Guests will hear directly from Taryn about her priorities for housing, transportation, small business growth, and neighborhood-focused planning, while engaging in an interactive experience that invites attendees to share the issues that matter most to them.
