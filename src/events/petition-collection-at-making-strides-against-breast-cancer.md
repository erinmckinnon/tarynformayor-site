## Petition Collection at Making Strides Against Breast Cancer

Saturday, October 24, 2026 • 7:00–10:00 AM

Join Team Taryn Sabia to help collect petition signatures at Making Strides Against Breast Cancer. Spend a three-hour volunteer shift connecting with residents and supporting Taryn’s campaign for Tampa Mayor.

To volunteer, email [contact@tarynformayor.com](mailto:contact@tarynformayor.com) and let us know you’d like to help collect petition signatures at Making Strides on October 24. Please contact the team for the meeting location and volunteer details.
