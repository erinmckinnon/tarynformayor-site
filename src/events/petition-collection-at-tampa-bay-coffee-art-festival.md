## Petition Collection at Tampa Bay Coffee & Art Festival

Saturday, October 24, 2026 • 10:00 AM–1:00 PM

Join Team Taryn Sabia to help collect petition signatures at the Tampa Bay Coffee & Art Festival. Spend a three-hour volunteer shift connecting with residents and supporting Taryn’s campaign for Tampa Mayor.

**Florida State Fairgrounds**
Tampa, FL
Volunteer meeting details will be provided upon signup.

To volunteer, email [contact@tarynformayor.com](mailto:contact@tarynformayor.com) and let us know you’d like to help collect petition signatures on October 24.
