## Vote by Mail
Request Deadline

February 18, 2027 • 5:00 PM Eastern

Planning to vote by mail in Tampa’s upcoming election? February 18 is the final day to request that a vote-by-mail ballot be mailed to you. Submit your request to the Hillsborough County Supervisor of Elections by **5:00 PM Eastern**.

[Request My Ballot](https://www.votehillsborough.gov/173/Request-a-Vote-By-Mail-Ballot) [Vote-by-Mail Information](https://www.votehillsborough.gov/169/Vote-By-Mail)

This is the deadline to request a mailed ballot. Completed ballots must be received by the Supervisor of Elections by 7:00 PM on Election Day.
