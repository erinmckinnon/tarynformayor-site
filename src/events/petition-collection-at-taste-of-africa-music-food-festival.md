## Petition Collection at Taste of Africa Music & Food Festival

Saturday, October 24, 2026 • 2:00–5:00 PM

Join Team Taryn Sabia to help collect petition signatures at Taste of Africa Music & Food Festival. Spend a three-hour volunteer shift connecting with residents and supporting Taryn’s campaign for Tampa Mayor.

To volunteer, email [contact@tarynformayor.com](mailto:contact@tarynformayor.com) and let us know you’d like to help collect petition signatures at Taste of Africa on October 24. Please contact the team for the meeting location and volunteer details.
