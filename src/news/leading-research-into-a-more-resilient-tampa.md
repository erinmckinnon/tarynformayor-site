Before running for mayor, Taryn Sabia was already helping shape Tampa’s future through research. As part of a partnership between the University of South Florida and the City of Tampa, she led work on Tampa’s first **Heat Resilience Playbook**, using data to identify neighborhoods most vulnerable to extreme heat and developing practical strategies such as expanding tree canopy, creating cooling spaces, and improving long-term resilience. It’s an example of Taryn’s approach to leadership—bringing research, community engagement, and smart planning together to solve real problems facing our city.
