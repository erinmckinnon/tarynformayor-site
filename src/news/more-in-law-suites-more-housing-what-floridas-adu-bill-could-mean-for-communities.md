#### Coverage in Bay News 9 | November 28, 2025

Taryn Sabia was featured in Bay News 9 discussing Florida’s proposed legislation to expand accessory dwelling units (ADUs), often called in-law suites or backyard cottages, as a strategy to address housing affordability and neighborhood growth.

The proposal would require local governments across Florida to allow ADUs in single-family neighborhoods, potentially creating more flexible housing options while raising important questions about infrastructure, flooding risk, and neighborhood character.

Drawing on her experience in urban planning and community development, Sabia noted that ADUs can help neighborhoods evolve while preserving their historic identity and supporting a broader range of housing affordability. She emphasized that implementation matters, stressing the importance of aligning new housing tools with local infrastructure capacity and environmental conditions.

Her perspective reflects a balanced approach to growth — expanding housing choices while ensuring thoughtful planning that protects neighborhood stability and long-term resilience.
