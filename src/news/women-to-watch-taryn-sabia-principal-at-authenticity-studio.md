#### Featured in Tampa Magazine | Women to Watch

Taryn Sabia was recognized by Tampa Magazine as a “Women to Watch,” highlighting her leadership in urban planning, research, and community-centered city development.

The profile notes her role as Assistant Dean for Research at the University of South Florida’s College of Design, Art & Performance and her leadership of AuthentiCITY Studio, a consulting practice focused on shaping vibrant, resilient cities.

Her work centers on collaborative urban strategies that bridge local needs with global best practices — advancing equitable growth, sustainable development, and community-driven design across Tampa and beyond.

This recognition reflects her long-standing commitment to thoughtful planning, strong partnerships, and building cities that are more livable, connected, and resilient for future generations.
