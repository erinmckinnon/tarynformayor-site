## A Campaign Built With Tampa

Building a people powered campaign starts with people. Right now, one of the most important ways you can help is by signing and circulating a candidate petition for Taryn Sabia.

Every valid signature moves us closer to qualifying for the ballot and gives us another opportunity to connect with someone who cares about Tampa’s future.

Signing a petition does not mean you are promising to vote for Taryn, make a donation, or endorse every part of her platform. It simply means you believe Tampa voters should have the opportunity to hear her ideas and consider her name on the ballot.

## Four Ways You Can Help

Whether you collect one signature or twenty, you are helping build this campaign from the ground up.

### Sign a Petition

Add your name and help Taryn take another important step toward the ballot.

### Ask Your Network

Invite registered Tampa voters among your friends, family, neighbors, and coworkers to sign.

### Carry Petitions

Bring a few forms to your next neighborhood gathering, community meeting, or coffee date.

### Volunteer With Us

Help collect signatures at campaign events and community gatherings across Tampa.

## Why This Matters

Tampa is growing quickly, and the decisions we make now will shape our neighborhoods for generations. Taryn is running for mayor because she believes growth should strengthen our communities, preserve what makes Tampa special, and create opportunities for everyone who calls this city home.

This campaign is about listening to residents, bringing people together, and building a city that works better for all of us. The petition process is one of the first ways Tampa voters can take part.
