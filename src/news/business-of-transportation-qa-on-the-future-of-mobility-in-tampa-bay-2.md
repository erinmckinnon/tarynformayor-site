#### Coverage in Tampa Bay Business Journal | November 1, 2019

Taryn Sabia was featured in the Tampa Bay Business Journal’s “Business of Transportation” series, discussing key issues shaping the future of mobility and urban development in the Tampa Bay region.

The Q&A highlights the importance of integrated transportation planning, smart growth strategies, and forward-thinking infrastructure investments to support the region’s continued growth. As a long-time urban planning professional and researcher at the University of South Florida, Taryn emphasized the need for coordinated regional approaches that connect land use, transit, and economic development.

Her perspective reflects a commitment to building a more connected, accessible, and resilient Tampa — where transportation systems support opportunity, reduce congestion, and enhance quality of life for residents across all neighborhoods.
