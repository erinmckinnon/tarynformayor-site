#### University of South Florida News | December 3, 2024

Taryn Sabia participated in a regional forum hosted by the University of South Florida that brought together emergency management officials, planners, and civic leaders to assess hurricane preparedness and recovery efforts across the Tampa Bay region.

The event, focused on lessons learned from recent storms, explored how communities responded to multiple hurricanes and how the region can strengthen resilience moving forward. Discussions emphasized the importance of coordinated planning, infrastructure readiness, and regional collaboration to better protect residents and accelerate recovery after major storm events.

Sabia joined other public sector and regional planning leaders to evaluate long-term strategies that improve preparedness, strengthen community resilience, and ensure that future growth is aligned with climate and infrastructure realities. Her participation reflects ongoing engagement in shaping data-driven, forward-looking policies that help Tampa and the broader region adapt to increasing storm risks.
