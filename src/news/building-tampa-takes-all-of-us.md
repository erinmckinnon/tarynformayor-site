A People-Powered Campaign

Tampa’s future will not be shaped by one candidate, one campaign, or one election. It will be shaped by the people who live here, and by the choices we make together.

<div class="embed"><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/jWTukB4nwI8" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

Every day, I hear from residents who love Tampa but worry about where we are headed. Families are struggling with rising costs. Longtime residents are watching their neighborhoods change without feeling included in the decisions.

People want safer streets, better transportation choices, stronger neighborhoods, and a city government that listens before it acts.

These challenges are significant, but so is our ability to address them, if we build a campaign powered by people instead of politics as usual.

We are building this campaign one conversation, one volunteer, and one contribution at a time.

Get Involved

## This Is a People-Powered Campaign

You do not need political experience to be part of it. You do not need to have hours of free time or the ability to make a large donation.

You simply need to believe that Tampa deserves thoughtful leadership, responsible growth, and a city government that works for every neighborhood.

Choose the way you want to help:

01 Join us at a community event and bring a friend.   02 Volunteer for a neighborhood canvass.   03 Help make calls or send messages to Tampa voters.   04 Host a small gathering so your friends and neighbors can meet Taryn.   05 Share our campaign with the people in your network.   06 Make a contribution of any amount.

Every one of these actions helps us reach another person who may not yet know that there is a different kind of leadership possible for Tampa.

Invest in Tampa’s Future

## Why Your Contribution Matters

Running a citywide campaign requires resources. Contributions help us print campaign materials, communicate with voters, organize events, equip volunteers, and bring our message into neighborhoods throughout Tampa.

But the value of a contribution is about more than its dollar amount. Each donation shows that another person believes in this campaign and is willing to invest in Tampa’s future.

A campaign supported by many individual donors is accountable to the community it hopes to serve. That is the kind of campaign we are committed to building.

Everyone Belongs

## There Is a Place for You Here

This campaign is not only for people who already follow local government closely. It is for parents thinking about the city their children will inherit. It is for small-business owners, renters, homeowners, students, retirees, neighborhood advocates, and anyone who believes Tampa can grow without losing what makes our communities special.

Whether you can give an hour, host a conversation, share a post, or make a small donation, your participation matters.
