#### Coverage in Tampa Bay 28 | Local News

Taryn Sabia was featured in Tampa Bay 28 discussing new research showing that increasing flood risk in the Tampa Bay region will disproportionately impact lower-income and historically underserved communities.

The report highlights how rising sea levels, stronger storms, and aging infrastructure are expected to intensify flooding risks across the region in the coming decades. Researchers noted that vulnerable communities are often located in low-lying areas and have historically received less investment in flood mitigation and maintenance, making them less equipped to recover from disasters.

Drawing on her expertise in urban planning and community design, Sabia emphasized the importance of proactive resilience planning that considers both physical infrastructure and social vulnerability. She highlighted the need for strategies that protect existing neighborhoods, improve building resilience, and ensure equitable investment in flood mitigation efforts.

Her perspective reflects a commitment to data-driven planning that prepares Tampa for future climate risks while prioritizing the safety and stability of all communities.
