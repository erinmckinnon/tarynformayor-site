Taryn Sabia for Mayor of Tampa (“Campaign,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, events, volunteer activities, donations, and communications.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you visit our website, sign up for updates, make a donation, volunteer, or otherwise interact with the Campaign.

## Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide to us, including:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address
- Donation information
- Employer and occupation information (when required by law)
- Volunteer information
- Event registration details
- Survey responses and communications

We may also automatically collect certain information when you visit the website, including:

- IP address
- Browser type
- Device information
- Pages visited
- Referral sources
- Cookies and analytics data

## Donations and Campaign Finance Compliance

Campaign finance laws may require us to collect and report certain donor information. Depending on applicable law, contributions above certain thresholds may require disclosure of:

- Name
- Address
- Employer
- Occupation

This information may be reported to applicable election authorities as required by law.

Online donations may be processed through third-party fundraising platforms such as Anedot or other payment processors. Those services maintain their own privacy policies and security practices.

## How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

- Send campaign updates and newsletters
- Respond to inquiries
- Process donations
- Organize volunteer activities and events
- Improve website functionality and user experience
- Conduct analytics and outreach
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
- Send text messages or emails regarding campaign activities, fundraising, and voting information

## Text Messaging (SMS) Communications

If you opt in to receive text messages from the Campaign:

- Message frequency may vary
- Message and data rates may apply
- You may opt out at any time by replying “STOP”
- Reply “HELP” for assistance

Text messaging opt-in information and consent will not be sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to:

- Understand website traffic
- Improve performance
- Measure campaign effectiveness
- Personalize user experience

You may disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the website may not function properly.

We may use third-party analytics tools such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel to help understand website usage and advertising performance.

## Sharing of Information

We may share information with:

- Campaign staff, volunteers, consultants, and vendors
- Service providers assisting with fundraising, communications, or website operations
- Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law
- Election authorities for required campaign finance reporting

We do not sell your personal information.

## Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including donation platforms, social media platforms, or external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party websites.

## Children’s Privacy

This website is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Choices

You may:

- Unsubscribe from emails using the unsubscribe link in campaign emails
- Opt out of text messages by replying “STOP”
- Request updates or corrections to your information by contacting us

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
