How to update the website

Simple steps for the most common changes. You can’t break anything — every change can be undone.

Jump to: Add an event Change or cancel an event Post news or a press article Put a message across the top of the website Change words on the homepage Add a photo or flyer I made a mistake / I’m stuck

📅 Add an event

  1. On the left, click 📅 Events.
  2. Click the purple + Event button.
  3. Fill in the boxes from top to bottom: the name, then click the date box and pick the day and time.
  4. Pick the Type of event. Coffee & Conversations also show up on the Coffee page automatically.
  5. If people should RSVP, paste the Anedot or Mobilize link into RSVP or ticket link.
  6. Click Publish at the top right, then Publish now.

The website updates about 1 minute after you publish. Refresh tarynformayor.com to see it. Old events leave the homepage on their own — you never need to delete them.

✏️ Change or cancel an event

  1. Click 📅 Events. The newest events are at the top.
  2. Click the event you want to change.
  3. Change what you need, then click Publish → Publish now.
  4. To take it off the website, turn on Hide this event from the website at the bottom, then publish.

📰 Post news or a press article

  1. Click 📰 News & Updates, then + News post.
  2. Type the headline, pick the date and a category.
  3. For a newspaper article about Taryn: choose In the News, write a short paragraph, and paste the newspaper’s link into Link to the outside article.
  4. Add a picture if you have one (square works best).
  5. Click Publish → Publish now.

📣 Put a message across the top of the website

  1. Click ⚙️ Site Settings, then Announcement bar, donate link, contact info.
  2. Type your message in Announcement bar — for example “Early voting starts Feb 15!”
  3. Click Publish → Publish now.
  4. To remove it later, delete the words in that box and publish again.

🏠 Change words on the homepage

  1. Click 🏠 Homepage. Each item is one part of the homepage, in order from top to bottom.
  2. Click the part you want (for example 7. Donate section).
  3. Change the words, then click Publish → Publish now.

Please check with Erin before changing Taryn’s photos, the “Paid for by” line, or anything under Advanced.

🖼️ Add a photo or flyer

  1. In any photo box, click Choose an image.
  2. Click Upload and pick the picture from your computer (or drag it into the window).
  3. Click the picture, then Choose selected.

Phone photos are fine. Pictures from Canva work great too — download them as PNG or JPG first.

🆘 I made a mistake / I’m stuck

  1. If you haven’t published yet, just close the page — nothing changes until you click Publish.
  2. If you already published, open the same item, fix it, and publish again.
  3. Still stuck? Email contact@tarynformayor.com and tell us what you were trying to do. Every old version is saved, so anything can be put back.
